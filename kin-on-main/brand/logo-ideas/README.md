# Kin on Main logo ideas

Concepts for review, not final art.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `kin-on-main-storefront-light.png` | On Paper Cream: the app, receipts, window decals |
| `kin-on-main-storefront-dark.png` | On indigo: social, signage, dark mode |

**Concept:** one storefront with a striped gold awning, a brick shopfront, a rising sun in the
window and an indigo door with an "open" sign. The wordmark is "Kin *on* Main" with "on" in
italic brick (gold on dark), and the tagline is MAIN STREET, RISING TOGETHER.

**In the app:** `KinMark` (`src/components/ui.tsx`) and `public/favicon.svg` are SVG versions of
the storefront, simplified for small sizes. The full lockup shows on Welcome and About. Profile →
Logo ideas shows both concepts and the mark at 16–64px.

**Open questions for the designer**
- The brand guide describes a row of **three** storefronts; this concept has one.
- At 32px and below the "open" sign and the sun's rays don't read.
- Still needed: a one-color version, and tests at decal size (4 in) and favicon size (32px).
- In the dark version, the indigo awning bar and ground line almost disappear on navy.
